The Mithi

A Culinary Daydream in the Heart of Makati

In the heart of Makati's urban rhythm, Mithi Café + Bistro unfolds as a sanctuary where culinary desires take shape. Sunlight dances across intimate tables, while the murmur of the city fades into the background. Here, every sip and bite transforms simple moments into quiet magic—where cravings meet their perfect match.

Mithi (n.): the sweet fulfillment of what you've been longing for.

Meet The Chefs

Angelico Agulto

Master Chef

With a decade of culinary expertise rooted in Kamuning's vibrant food culture, Chef Angelico Agulto brings precision, passion, and professionalism to every dish. His 10-year journey through kitchens has honed a mastery of technique and a deep understanding of balanced flavors.

Trained in both classic and contemporary methods, Chef Agulto approaches cooking with discipline and attention to detail. His leadership in the kitchen reflects a commitment to excellence, from ingredient selection to final presentation.

Avril Balington

Barista

A seasoned coffee professional with eight years of specialty coffee experience, Avril Balington brings technical expertise and warm hospitality to every cup. Her meticulous approach to extraction and flavor profiling ensures consistently excellent brews, while her engaging service creates memorable coffee experiences.

Having trained numerous baristas in Cavite's specialty coffee scene, Avril is recognized for both her craftsmanship and commitment to sharing knowledge. Her dedication to quality and customer experience continues to elevate coffee culture in the region.
